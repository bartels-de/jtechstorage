# Getting started

## Installation

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) **0.19.5 or newer** for Minecraft **26.3**.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) and `jtechstorage-all-<version>.jar` into your `mods` folder – or
   only the parts you want (Core, Helpers & Phoenix, Adventure; see [Modules](../../README.md#modules)).
3. Optional: [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) for recipe lookup and one-click recipe transfer into the Crafting Terminal.

Java **25** is required. On a server, the mod has to be installed on the server **and** on every client.

## Your first five minutes

1. **Craft a Storage Terminal and a Storage Key** (see [Recipes](13-recipes.md#storage-network)) and place the terminal.
   It belongs to you.
2. **Sneak + right-click the terminal** with the Storage Key. The key is now bound to this terminal.
3. **Sneak + right-click your chests, barrels and shulker boxes** with the key. Each one joins the network (do it again to
   remove it). A double chest counts as one container. While you hold the key, linked containers sparkle.
4. **Open the terminal.** You see every item from every linked container in one list, with exact counts and a search
   field.

That's the whole storage network. Everything else in the mod builds on top of it.

On your first join you receive the **Storage Handbook**. Jbartels, the creator of the mod, appears as a hologram and gives
you a short tour of the stations. Sneak + right-click with the handbook restarts the tour.

Later, he drops by briefly at big milestones – your first linked chest, your first sprite, the bosses, the Crystal
Depths, his workshop and a few more. Each appears once; right-click sends him away early. You can turn this off with
`milestoneHologram` in the [configuration](15-configuration.md).

## Roadmap

There is no fixed order, but this path leads you through the whole mod:

| Step | Goal | You get |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Terminal, key and your first chests | One searchable storage → [Storage network](02-storage-network.md) |
| 2 | Craft a **Resonance Igniter** and defeat the **Amethyst Robot** | Iron upgrades, **Amethyst Cores**, Wireless Cores → [Bosses](09-bosses-and-end.md#amethyst-robot) |
| 3 | Build your first helpers | Sprite, Watch Moth, Griffin, Spark Beetle, Chel, Ashley → [Helpers](04-helpers.md) |
| 4 | Team and Trade Terminals, phoenix mail | Play together, run a shop, send parcels → [Teams](03-teams-and-trade.md), [Phoenix](06-phoenix.md) |
| 5 | Explore amethyst geodes | A **companion** and a trader → [Companions](07-companions.md) |
| 6 | Evolve Chel with a nether star | **Peter Chel Parker**, your base protector → [Helpers](04-helpers.md#peter-chel-parker) |
| 7 | Mine amethyst and keep your base defended | The **Resonance Storm** comes to you → [Resonance Storm](08-resonance-storm.md) |
| 8 | Find a Crystal Lair in the End, defeat the **Great Amethyst Spider** | **Amethyst Obsidian Upgrades** → [Bosses](09-bosses-and-end.md#crystal-lair-and-great-amethyst-spider) |
| 9 | Amethyst Obsidian gear, Amethyst Phoenix, Amethyst Fox | Gear above netherite, travel, crystal enchantments |
| 10 | Craft a **Dragon Heart** | **Cheldrogan**, the crystal serpent dragon → [Cheldrogan](05-cheldrogan.md) |

## Tips

- Hold **Shift** over any mod item to see how it is used.
- The **Recipe Book** (book + amethyst shard + crafting table) shows every recipe in game.
- If something has no recipe, the [Recipes page](13-recipes.md#items-without-a-recipe) tells you where it comes from.
