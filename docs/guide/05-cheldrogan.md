# Cheldrogan

<img src="../images/items/cheldrogan.png" width="64" align="right">

Craft a **Dragon Heart** (dragon's breath, Crystal Obsidian, amethyst blocks, a nether star and an Amethyst Obsidian
Upgrade – see [Recipes](13-recipes.md#dragon-heart)) and right-click **Peter Chel Parker** with it. He rises as
**Cheldrogan**, a hovering crystal serpent dragon: a long, wingless body of amethyst scales with golden belly plates and
crystal spines, a glowing mane, branching crystal antlers, long whiskers, a crystal flame at the tip of his tail and a
resonance pearl floating in front of him.

## Riding

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Right-click | mount |
| Jump | take off; in the air, jump rises |
| Look around | he flies where you look |
| **Left click** | **Tail sweep** – he spins once around himself and hurls everything nearby away |
| **R** | **Crystal breath** – a beam that follows your gaze. Where it hits, resonance crystals grow from the ground and encase monsters for a few seconds |
| **V** | **Crystal shard rain** – he rears up and amethyst shards rain down where you aim |
| Sneak | dismount |

Breath and shard rain have short cooldowns (4 s and 10 s by default, see [Configuration](15-configuration.md)). Both
keys can be changed in the Minecraft controls. The resonance crystals disappear on their own and drop nothing. While
you ride him, left click neither breaks blocks nor hits with your hand.

## Guarding

Without a rider he guards the base like Peter does:

- shard rain against groups and distant monsters,
- tail sweep up close,
- crystal breath at range and against targets he cannot reach.

He heals at [Ashley](04-helpers.md#ashley). If you dismount far away from his base, that spot becomes his new base. At
night he coils up on the ground but stays on duty.

## Defeat

At 0 health the Dragon Heart burns out and he turns back into Peter Chel Parker. Health and damage can be set in the
[configuration](15-configuration.md#helpers-helpers) (`cheldroganHealth`, `cheldroganDamage`).
