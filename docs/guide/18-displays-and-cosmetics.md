# Network displays and cosmetics

Show off your storage on the wall, and show off yourself: wings, hats, trails and emotes that every player can see.

## Storage Display

A flat panel for walls that shows the network of a Storage Terminal live.

1. Place it on a wall.
2. Click it with a **Storage Key** that is bound to a terminal. You need at least the *view* permission in that network.
3. Click it with any **item** to choose what it shows – or right-click with an empty hand to open the **menu** (see below).
4. **Sneak + right-click with an empty hand** to switch the mode.

Several displays that face the same way form **one big screen**, up to **4 wide and 3 high**. The top-left block stores
the settings and draws the whole picture. Clicks on the other blocks are passed to it automatically. If you extend a
screen to the left or upwards, the new top-left block has to be linked again.

| Mode | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Item** | The item spins, with its stock below (it counts up smoothly) and the trend per minute. Wide screens put the item on the left and the numbers on the right. |
| **Fill level** | Used and total slots of the network as a bar. It turns yellow above 75 % and red above 90 %. |
| **History** | A curve of the stock over the last 10 minutes (one point every 10 seconds). On screens two or more blocks high, the fill level is shown above it. |
| **Top 5** | The five most common items in the network, with bars. Wider screens show their names too. |

The screen glows, and a small light in the corner shows the state: a green pulse means online, a red blink means the
terminal is missing or not linked. The heading uses the network name from the Team Terminal, if you set one.

## Storage Banner

A velvet banner on a golden rod that waves gently and hangs **two blocks down** from where you place it. Leave the
block below free.

1. Place it on a wall and link it with the Storage Key.
2. Click it with an item to put it on the ticker. Up to **8 items** fit.
3. Click again with the same item to take it off. **Sneak + empty hand** clears the banner.

Each line shows the item, its stock and a trend sign: **+** rising, **-** falling, **=** unchanged in the last minute.
With more than five items, the list rolls slowly upwards.

## The menu

Right-click a display or banner with an **empty hand** to open its menu. It has two tabs:

- **Display:** pick an item straight from the network – with a search field and a scrollable grid that shows the stock
  of each item. Switch the mode, take the item from your hand or clear the screen. On a banner, a click adds an item to
  the ticker; a click on an item on the banner takes it off again.
- **Look:** a live preview and all the options:

| Storage Display | Storage Banner |
|---|---|
| Frame: dark, amethyst, gold, copper, wood or no frame | Cloth colour: any of the 16 dye colours |
| Background: night, violet, ocean, forest, ember or black | Trim: gold, silver, copper, amethyst or none |
| Accent colour for bars, curves and numbers (8 colours) | Pattern: plain, stripes, rhombus, stars or crystals |
| Glow on or off | Text colour: cream, gold, white or dark |
| Scanlines on or off | Wind: still, light or strong |

Big screens are set up through any of their blocks; the settings always belong to the whole screen.

## Cosmetics

Press **J** to open the cosmetics menu. On the left you see your own figure: drag it with the mouse to turn it, so you
can see wings and capes from behind. On the right you pick a slot and click a card to put it on or take it off. Every
player on the server sees what you wear.

- Hats are hidden under a helmet or another head item. Only the **Crystal Halo** stays visible.
- Play your selected emote with **N**. Emotes have a 10 second cooldown.
- Locked cards show the advancement that unlocks them in the tooltip.
