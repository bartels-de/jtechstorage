# Bosses and the End

## Amethyst Robot

<img src="../images/items/amethyst_draught.png" width="64" align="right">

The first boss and the source of your first **Amethyst Cores**.

- **Summon it** with a **Resonance Igniter** ([recipe](13-recipes.md#resonance-igniter)). Only do it once you are well
  equipped!
- A huge mech with **400 health**: crystal fist, laser cannon, stomp and dash attacks. Below 50 % health it enters an
  **overload** phase.
- Without a target it powers down into standby.

| Loot | Chance |
|---|---|
| Range Upgrade (Iron) and Capacity Upgrade (Iron) | always – the only way to get iron upgrades |
| Amethyst Core **or** Wireless Core | 30 % |

The Amethyst Core is needed for every [helper](04-helpers.md). Health, armor, damage and the core chance are
[configurable](15-configuration.md).

## Crystal Lair and Great Amethyst Spider

<img src="../images/items/lair_compass.png" width="64" align="right">

**Crystal Lairs** are floating arenas in the **outer End**. Right-click a **Lair Compass** in the End and it points to the
nearest one.

- Your first visit awakens the **Great Amethyst Spider** (**600 health**): venom bites, leaps, crystal webs, lines of
  ground spikes and a resonance shriek. Below 50 % health she calls **Crystal Spiderlings**.
- Without a target she lurks low with her eyes half closed.
- **Summon her again** by placing a **Crystal Cocoon** on the **Crystal Altar** in the heart of the lair.
- Every victory drops **exactly one Amethyst Obsidian Upgrade**.

The upgrade is used for Amethyst Obsidian gear, to evolve a phoenix into the
[Amethyst Phoenix](06-phoenix.md#amethyst-phoenix), and in the [Dragon Heart](05-cheldrogan.md).

## The Last Storage Master

The boss of the Crystal Depths waits in the **Storage Masters' Temple**, a very rare structure on the cave floor (see
[The Crystal Depths](17-crystal-depths.md#the-storage-masters-temple)). Right-click the **seal** in the middle of the
temple with an **Amethyst Core** to wake him. The core is used up; you get one back when he dies.

| | |
|---|---|
| Health / armour | 500 / 10, immune to fire and knockback |
| Phase 1 (above 2/3) | Floats and keeps his distance, throws fans of 3 crystal shards, blinks away when you get close |
| Phase 2 (above 1/3) | Calls 2 Crystal Lurkers and 3 Swarm Shards. While any servant lives, a seal blocks **75 %** of the damage – defeat the servants first |
| Phase 3 | Fans of 5 shards, a rain of shards around you, and a shockwave (9 damage, strong knockback) when you stand within 6 blocks |
| Loot | **Storage Master's Hammer**, 4–7 pure amethyst, an Amethyst Core, 500 XP |

He talks to everyone nearby while he fights. Defeating him gives the challenge **Closing Time**.

### Storage Master's Hammer

A pickaxe above netherite level that mines **3 × 3** at once (sneak to mine a single block). Only blocks you could mine
with a pickaxe are taken along. Bind it to a terminal (sneak + right-click the terminal) and everything you mine goes
**straight into your storage** while you are within wireless range.

## Amethyst Obsidian gear

<img src="../images/items/amethyst_obsidian_chestplate.png" width="64" align="right">

Stronger than netherite and fireproof. Made at the **smithing table**:

**Amethyst Obsidian Upgrade + netherite piece + Crystal Obsidian** – one upgrade per piece, enchantments are kept.
**Crystal Obsidian** (crying obsidian, amethyst blocks and netherite scrap) is also the repair material.

| | Netherite | Amethyst Obsidian |
|---|---:|---:|
| Armor (helmet / chestplate / leggings / boots) | 3 / 8 / 6 / 3 | 4 / 9 / 7 / 4 |
| Toughness / knockback resistance | 3 / 0.1 | 4 / 0.15 |
| Tool durability / mining speed | 2031 / 9 | 2700 / 11 |
| Sword damage | 8 | 9 |
| Enchantability | 15 | 18 |

**Full set bonus (all four armor pieces):** +2 hearts, Fire Resistance and Haste I, plus **Resonance Thorns**: melee
attackers take 3 + 25 % of the damage back as crystal damage and are slowed.

## Crystal Forge

<img src="../images/items/crystal_forge.png" width="64" align="right">

A workshop block that makes **netherite** and **Amethyst Obsidian** gear (and the Storage Master's Hammer) even better.
Put the piece in the top slot and the material below; each button shows the current level and what the next one costs.

| Upgrade | Levels | Effect per level | Fits | Material for level 1 / 2 / 3 |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Hardened** | 3 | +25 % durability | everything | 2 / 4 / 6 pure amethyst |
| **Sharpened** | 3 | +1 attack damage, faster mining | tools and weapons | 4 / 8 / 12 glow crystals |
| **Reinforced** | 3 | +1 armor, +0.5 toughness | armor | 1 / 2 / 3 netherite scrap |
| **Self-Mending** | 1 | repairs 1 durability every 5 seconds while you carry or wear it | everything | 1 netherite ingot |

- Upgrades show in the tooltip and stay when you repair, enchant or rename the piece.
- Upgrading a forged netherite piece to Amethyst Obsidian keeps the upgrades, and the stats are recalculated for the new
  piece.

**Recipe:** 3 pure amethyst on top, polished deepslate – smithing table – polished deepslate in the middle and polished
deepslate – anvil – polished deepslate below.
