# The Crystal Depths

A dimension of its own, deep below the world: huge crystal caves up to 190 blocks high without a sky. The only light comes
from glowing crystals, veins in the rock and rivers of lava.

## The Amethyst Portal

1. Build a frame of **amethyst blocks** like a Nether portal. The inside must be at least 2 wide and 3 high (up to 21 × 21).
   The corners are not needed.
2. **Right-click the frame with an Amethyst Core.** The core is not used up, so one core lights every portal you build.
3. Step into the swirling portal and wait a moment.

The scale is 1:1: you arrive at the same X and Z. If there is no portal within 64 blocks on the other side, a new one is
built on a small platform, and it takes you back. Breaking the frame closes the portal.

### Linking portals

You can connect two portals for good, no matter how far apart they are:

1. Right-click a portal (or its amethyst frame) in the Overworld with a **Storage Key**. The key remembers it.
2. Right-click a portal in the Crystal Depths with the same key. Both portals are now linked and always lead to each
   other.

**Sneak + right-click** with the key removes the link. If one of the portals is broken, the link disappears and the
portal goes back to finding the nearest portal.

## Biomes

Each biome has its own cave shape, its own colours, fog, light and sounds.

| Biome | Shape | What you find |
|---|---|---|
| **Geode Halls** | High, vaulted halls | Giant geodes (some with a glowing core), crystal pillars, big crystal stalactites and stalagmites, floating shards |
| **Crystal Lakes** | Large lakes low in the caves | Turquoise water, resonance moss banks, crystal sprouts, waterfalls |
| **Vein Chasm** | Solid rock cut by deep, narrow canyons | Dark vein stone, lava rivers at the bottom of the canyons, lava falls – the most dangerous part |
| **Resonance Grotto** | Low, wide caves | Moss on floor and ceiling, giant **resonance mushrooms** with bouncy caps, glowing shrooms, fireflies, thick fog |
| **Prism Forest** | A wide hall full of rock pillars | **Crystal turf** with crystal grass, slender crystal spires in azure, rose and citrine, colourful crystal clusters |
| **Rose Quartz Gardens** | Wide open halls | **Rose moss** and crystal turf, **rose quartz blooms**, rose crystal spires, falling petals |
| **Azure Frost** | Icy caves | Slippery **frost crystal** floors, frozen lakes, azure icicles and stalactites, snowflakes |
| **Citrine Dunes** | A huge hall over a wavy sand floor | **Citrine sand** dunes over citrine sandstone, citrine spires and clusters, warm dusty air |
| **Floating Expanse** | An enormous void | **Floating islands** with crystal turf, stalactites underneath, many waterfalls into a lake far below |
| **Crystal Plains** | A huge flat hall with a very high, vaulted ceiling | Level **crystal turf** floor at about y 44, more than 50 blocks of air above, only a few pillars, soft light, very few monsters, plenty of crystal deer and geode sheep – made for building |

Biomes no longer change at a hard edge: the cave shape blends over a wider band, and floor, ceiling and plants of
neighbouring biomes mix over about 16–24 blocks (moss runs into turf, sand into frost and so on).

Beds and respawn anchors don't work in the Crystal Depths. The dimension is dark on purpose: light comes from single
crystals, mushrooms and lava, so bring torches or lanterns.

## Creatures

Only the creatures of the depths live here – no vanilla monsters spawn.

| Creature | Where | What it does | Drops |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crystal Lurker** | Most biomes | Waits curled up like a rock. Comes alive when you get within 6 blocks or hit it, smashes with both fists and knocks you back (40 health, armour). Curls up again without a target. | Crystal stone, amethyst, sometimes glow crystal |
| **Swarm Shard** | Most biomes | Small flying shards in swarms of 3–5. Weak but fast, dive at you and dart away. Hit one and the swarm joins in. | Amethyst |
| **Lake Wraith** | Crystal Lakes, Floating Expanse | Glowing jellyfish creature. Only attacks you in the water, grabs you and drags you down (Slowness). Dries out on land. | Glow ink sacs |
| **Vein Crawler** | Vein Chasm, Citrine Dunes | Six-legged, immune to fire and lava. Spits glowing crystal shards from a distance that set you on fire. | Amethyst, magma cream, sometimes vein stone |
| **Crystal Snail** | Geode Halls, Crystal Lakes, Resonance Grotto, Prism Forest, Rose Quartz Gardens | Peaceful and very slow. Feed it amethyst shards: a few minutes later it sheds 2–4 shards (sometimes a glow crystal); two fed snails breed. Hides in its shell when hit. | Amethyst |
| **Glow Fish** | Crystal Lakes, Floating Expanse | Small glowing schooling fish. Can't be caught in a bucket. | Glowstone dust |
| **Quokka** | Prism Forest, Rose Quartz Gardens | A small, smiling marsupial with amethyst crystals on its back. See below. | Sometimes amethyst |
| **Crystal Deer** | Prism Forest, Geode Halls, Rose Quartz Gardens | A mount with glowing crystal antlers. See below. | Leather, amethyst |
| **Geode Sheep** | Prism Forest, Rose Quartz Gardens, Resonance Grotto, Azure Frost | Grows softly glowing crystal wool. See below. | Mutton, sometimes amethyst |
| **Crystal Crab** | Crystal Lakes, Citrine Dunes, Resonance Grotto, Azure Frost | Scuttles along the shores and digs for small treasures. See below. | Sometimes amethyst |

All ten have spawn eggs in the creative tab.

### The Quokka

- **Taming:** feed it **grass** (short grass, tall grass, fern or crystal grass). Each bite has a 1 in 3 chance to tame it.
- **Healing and breeding:** grass also heals a tamed quokka, and two fed quokkas have a baby, which belongs to you as well.
- **Sitting:** right-click your quokka to make it sit or follow you again.
- **Curing zombie villagers:** a tamed quokka that is not sitting hops over to zombie villagers within 16 blocks and starts
  curing them, just like a splash potion of weakness and a golden apple would. The cure only takes 15–30 seconds. You
  count as the healer, so the cured villager gives you the usual discount. After a cure the quokka rests for about a minute.

### Crystal Deer, Geode Sheep and Crystal Crab

- **Crystal Deer:** tame it with **glow berries** (each one has a 1 in 3 chance) or by riding it, then put on a **saddle**.
  It jumps almost five blocks high, takes little fall damage and its hooves ring like amethyst. Glow berries heal it and
  let two deer have a fawn.
- **Geode Sheep:** shear it for **crystal wool** in its colour, a faintly glowing block (light 2). Dye the sheep like a
  normal one. Its wool grows back when it grazes on crystal turf or moss. Wheat, glow berries and crystal grass attract it.
- **Crystal Crab:** tame it with **fish** (cod, salmon or tropical fish). Now and then it digs in sand, gravel, dirt, turf or moss and
  drops what it finds: shards, nuggets, glow berries and sometimes something rare. A tamed crab digs much more often and
  sits on right-click.
- **Glow berries** now also drop from crystal vines (30 %).
- **Crystal wool** can be dyed (crystal wool + dye) and turned back into string.

## Pure Amethyst

**Pure Amethyst Ore** grows in the crystal stone of the whole dimension (Y 6–120). The **Vein Chasm** has a richer variant in
vein stone with larger deposits. Both shimmer slightly, need an **iron pickaxe** and drop experience.

| Step | How |
|---|---|
| Mine the ore | Drops **Raw Pure Amethyst**; Fortune gives more, Silk Touch keeps the ore |
| Smelt it | Furnace or blast furnace ➜ **Pure Amethyst** |
| Store it | 9 pure amethyst ➜ **Block of Pure Amethyst** (light 6), and back |

Pure amethyst is used for the two strongest network upgrades:

| Upgrade | Recipe (crafting table) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Range Upgrade (Crystal)** | Ender Upgrade in the middle, glow crystal at the sides, pure amethyst in the corners | Range 192 blocks; keeps the ender abilities (works across dimensions) |
| **Capacity Upgrade (Crystal)** | Capacity Upgrade (Netherite) in the middle, glow crystal at the sides, pure amethyst in the corners | 256 slots |

Both values can be changed in the config (`crystalRange`, `crystalCapacity`).

## Structures

The Storage Masters, the people who built the first storage networks down here, left ruins behind.

| Structure | Where | What you find |
|---|---|---|
| **Storage Ruin** | Geode Halls, Vein Chasm, Prism Forest, Azure Frost, Citrine Dunes – on the ground | A crumbling hall of crystal bricks in three forms: a **hall** with barrel shelves, a workbench and carpet remains; **two rooms** with an archive full of bookshelves and a lectern behind the front room; or a **hall with a tower** whose ladder leads up to a chest. Always: a broken terminal, chests, lanterns, cobwebs and rubble. |
| **Sunken Hall** | Crystal Lakes, on the lake floor | The same ruin, flooded. Take something to breathe. |
| **Crystal Shrine** | Most biomes, rare | A raised platform with four glowing pillars and a chest on a pedestal. In high caves every second one is a **pavilion** with a stepped roof, a glow crystal spire and a hanging lantern. Better loot. |
| **Jbartels' Workshop** | Every biome except the Vein Chasm and the Floating Expanse, on the cave floor, rare | See below. |
| **Crystal Haven** | Every biome except the Vein Chasm and the Floating Expanse (also the Crystal Plains), on the cave floor, very rare | The abandoned settlement of the Storage Masters. See below. |
| **Crystal Mine** | Every biome except the Floating Expanse, buried in the rock, very rare | Tunnels, rails and a hidden crystal vein. See below. |
| **Storage Masters' Temple** | Floor biomes, extremely rare | A pillared hall with the boss seal, an archive and a treasury. See below. |

The chests hold amethyst, glow crystal, pure amethyst, network upgrades, crystal building blocks – and often an
**Ancient Note**.

## Jbartels' Workshop

The abandoned workshop of B., the last Storage Master – better known as Jbartels, the creator of the mod. A half-timbered
house with a slate roof and a crystal rose window, often dug into a slope. It is dark inside, lit only by candles and two
soul lanterns.

- **Outside:** a half-dismantled giant robot with a terminal in its belly (*Prototype #4: Robot – ran away. Please
  report!*), a building site with scaffolding and an outdoor workbench.
- **Inside:** three glass cases with frozen prototypes of the Amethyst Sprite, the Watch Moth and the Spark Beetle, each
  with a sign; *Terminal v0.1* on a pedestal; a sparking Mega Terminal you should not switch on; workbenches, a lectern
  with B.'s notebook, a tool wall, an office with bookshelves, a pin board, a desk and a bed.
- The barrels hold tinkering junk: nuggets, redstone, amethyst shards, paper, a comparator or repeater.
- The prototypes cannot be tamed, linked or hurt.

Finding it gives the advancement **Behind the Scenes**, and Jbartels' hologram drops by.

## Crystal Haven

The settlement where the Storage Masters lived. It is about 44 × 44 blocks big and lies on the cave floor around a plaza
with a fountain and lantern posts.

- Three half-timbered houses with gable roofs, some of them half fallen in, with beds, barrels and chests.
- A half-collapsed **watchtower** with a ladder, a well and small gardens.
- An **archive** with bookshelf walls, a lectern and a chest of notes.
- Three **market stalls** with crystal wool awnings, counters and barrels.
- A **berry arbour** with hanging glow berries over moss beds.
- Furnished houses: a living room with table and chairs, a workshop with anvil and grindstone, a garden shed.
- A big **storage hall** with barrel shelves, a broken terminal and the best chests of the settlement. A **memory
  crystal** hangs in an item frame on its back wall.
- The chests hold amethyst, glow berries, crystal wool, crystal lanterns, network upgrades, saddles – and Ancient Notes.

Finding it gives the advancement **Crystal Haven**.

## Crystal Mine

An old mine deep inside the rock. You usually find it by digging or when a cave breaks into one of its tunnels.

- Four **tunnels** with dark oak frames, rails, soul lanterns and cobwebs. The walls are full of pure amethyst ore and
  coloured crystals.
- A **pit hall** in the middle with a shaft, a crane with a chain and cauldron, a workbench and barrels.
- Chest minecarts with mining loot: raw ores, coal, rails, soul torches, crystals and sometimes TNT or diamonds.
- A partly **collapsed tunnel** you have to crawl through, and a **tool room** off the west tunnel.
- The shaft leads down to a **lower level**: a ring gallery with even more ore, a flooded dip, the **miners'
  quarters** with beds and a **powder store** with TNT and a chest.
- Behind one of the tunnels lies a hidden **crystal vein chamber** full of amethyst and coloured crystals. Its chest always
  holds the **mine's memory crystal**.

Finding it gives the advancement **Good Luck Below!**.

Both new structures are rare on purpose: expect 800 blocks or more to the next one.

## The Storage Masters' Temple

The largest structure of the Depths and very rare (expect roughly 1000 blocks or more to the next one). A broad staircase
leads up to a pillared hall with a vaulted roof and a skylight. In the middle stands a raised dais with the
**Storage Masters' Seal**, framed by four glowing pillars and candles. At the back an altar with two crystal-headed
statues and two chests; to the sides an **archive** (bookshelves, lectern, chest) and a **treasury** (chests, barrels,
blocks of pure amethyst and gold).

The chests hold diamonds, pure amethyst, diamond upgrades, enchanted books, golden apples, netherite scrap and notes.
Finding the temple gives the advancement **Hallowed Halls**. Right-click the seal with an Amethyst Core to wake the
**Last Storage Master** – see [Bosses](09-bosses-and-end.md#the-last-storage-master).

## Memory Crystals

Twelve glowing crystals tell of the places of the Crystal Depths. They are collectibles – and look great in the
**Memory Case**.

| Memory crystal | Where |
|---|---|
| Geode Halls, Crystal Lakes, Vein Chasm, Resonance Grotto, Prism Forest, Rose Quartz Gardens, Azure Frost, Citrine Dunes, Floating Expanse | Standing on a chiseled pedestal on the cave floor of its biome (rare). Mine it to pick it up. |
| Workshop | In an item frame above the bookshelves in Jbartels' Workshop (where the compass used to hang) |
| Crystal Haven | In an item frame in the storage hall of the settlement |
| Crystal Mine | In the chest of the crystal vein chamber |

**Memory Case:** a wooden display case with glass sides and two shelves. Right-click it with a memory crystal to put it
in; the crystals float on the shelves. Right-click with an empty hand shows how many you have (for example *7/12*).
Sneak + empty hand takes the last one out again. Breaking the case drops all crystals.

| Recipe | |
|---|---|
| Top row | 3 dark oak slabs |
| Middle row | crystal glass, amethyst shard, crystal glass |
| Bottom row | gold ingot, polished crystal stone, gold ingot |

Advancements: **Do You Remember?** for your first memory crystal and the challenge **All the Memories** for a full
case (500 XP). The challenge also unlocks the **Memory Glow** aura cosmetic.

## The Storage Masters' notes

There are **10 Ancient Notes** to find in ruin, shrine and settlement chests. Right-click a note to read it. Together they tell what
happened to the Storage Masters, from their arrival in the Depths to their last message to whoever finds the way after them.
Each note you read is counted in the advancement **The Whole Story**, which is complete once you have read all ten.
