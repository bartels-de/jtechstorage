# Crystal enchantments

## Amethyst Fox

<img src="../images/items/amethyst_fox.png" width="64" align="right">

The **Amethyst Fox** is a crystal kitsune with three glowing tails – and the only source of crystal enchantments.

1. Place the fox and link it to your network with a bound Storage Key.
2. About **every three in-game days** it digs in dirt, sand or gravel during the day and finds an **enchanted book** with
   a crystal enchantment.
3. The book goes **straight into your storage**.

**Feeding** speeds it up, up to four times per day:

| Food | Time saved |
|---|---|
| Glow berries | 6 hours |
| Sweet berries | 3 hours |

Sneak + right-click with an empty hand packs it up. The dig interval and the feeding limit are
[configurable](15-configuration.md).

## The enchantments

These enchantments are **not** available from the enchanting table, villagers or loot. Apply the books with an anvil.

| Enchantment | Applies to | Levels | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Double Jump** | boots | I–II | one extra jump in mid-air per level |
| **Sprint** | leggings | I–III | +12 % speed per level while sprinting |
| **Recall** | Storage Key | I | right-click in the air to return to your terminal, once per day |
| **Careful Touch** | pickaxe | I | mines monster spawners (the mob is kept) and budding amethyst |
| **Life Spark** | sword, axe | I–III | melee hits heal you for 10 % of the damage per level (max. 2 hearts) |

### Quality of life

| Enchantment | Applies to | Levels | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Long Reach** | pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe | I–III | +1/2/3 blocks of reach while you hold the tool |
| **Soulbound** | any enchantable item | I–III | stays in your inventory when you die; the level drops by one each time (at 0 the enchantment is gone). Not combinable with Curse of Vanishing |
| **Restock** | Storage Key | I | when the stack in your hand runs out (blocks, food, torches …), it is refilled from your inventory or – bound and in wireless range – from your storage |

### Armor

| Enchantment | Applies to | Levels | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crystal Shield** | chestplate | I–III | after 10 seconds without damage you build up to 2/4/6 absorption hearts (half a heart per second) |
| **Nourishing** | helmet | I–III | 20/40/60 % less hunger |
| **Arrow Ward** | helmet | I–III | 20/40/60 % chance that arrows and other projectiles bounce off |

### Bow and crossbow

| Enchantment | Applies to | Levels | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wind Arrow** | bow, crossbow | I–III | arrows fly 15/30/45 % faster (same damage) and drop 30/50/70 % less |
| **Binding Arrow** | bow, crossbow | I–III | hits slow the target heavily (Slowness III) for 2/3/4 seconds |
| **Resonance Arrow** | bow, crossbow | I–III | every third hit on the same target echoes half a second later with 50/75/100 % extra damage |

### Melee

| Enchantment | Applies to | Levels | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beheading** | sword, axe | I–III | 5/10/15 % chance that zombies, skeletons, wither skeletons, creepers, piglins and players drop their head |
| **Crystal Venom** | sword | I–III | hits make the target bleed for 3 seconds (1/2/3 damage per second) |
| **Whirlwind** | sword, axe | I–III | a fully charged hit while sneaking also hits every monster within 2.5/3.5/4.5 blocks for 50/75/100 % of the damage |
