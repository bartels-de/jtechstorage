# Books and interface

## Books

Every book is crafted shapeless from **book + amethyst shard** (+ one more ingredient):

| | Book | Extra ingredient | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| <img src="../images/items/guide_book.png" width="32"> | **Storage Handbook** | – | basics, terminal, upgrades, teams, phoenix, backpacks, companions, controls |
| <img src="../images/items/recipe_book.png" width="32"> | **Recipe Book** | crafting table | every recipe, sorted by chapter |
| <img src="../images/items/bestiary.png" width="32"> | **Bestiary** | feather | all helpers, evolutions and bosses with a 3D model that follows your mouse, and their health |
| <img src="../images/items/item_lexicon.png" width="32"> | **Item Lexicon** | item frame | every block, tool, material and piece of gear explained |
| <img src="../images/items/crystal_chronicle.png" width="32"> | **Crystal Chronicle** | ender eye | the legend, bosses, Amethyst Obsidian, crystal enchantments, evolutions and a roadmap |
| <img src="../images/items/joke_book.png" width="32"> | **Chel's Joke Book** | cookie | collects every joke Chel and Peter tell you |

- You get the Storage Handbook on your first join. Sneak + right-click with it restarts Jbartels' hologram tour.
- Every book has a clickable table of contents; **☰** at the top left jumps back to it.
- Chel gives you your first joke book with his first joke. There are **75 jokes**; collecting all of them unlocks an
  advancement.

## Interface settings

The **gear button** in the Storage Terminal (below the upgrade slots) opens the settings. They apply to all screens of the
mod and are saved per player in `config/jtechstorage-client.json`. A live preview on the right shows every change at once.

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Theme | classic light, classic dark, frost glass, crystal glass, modern light, modern dark |
| Accent color | amethyst, team color, blue, green, red, gold, rose, teal, gray |
| Effects | preset *off*, *subtle* (default) or *rich* – or switch each effect on its own |
| Terminal height | 4, 6, 8 or 10 rows, or *auto* (as many as fit on the screen) |
| Item count size | small, normal, large |

**Themes.** *Classic* looks like vanilla. *Glass* themes are slightly see-through, with a glowing accent border and a soft
glow at the top. *Modern* themes are flat with rounded pixel corners and a soft shadow. Tooltips in the mod's screens match
the theme.

**Effects** (each can be turned on and off):

| Effect | What it does |
|---|---|
| Slide-in | windows glide gently into place when opened |
| Hover glow | slots and buttons under the mouse glow in the accent color |
| Border shimmer | a spark of light slowly travels around the window border |
| Sheen | every few seconds a streak of light sweeps across the window |
| Background particles | crystal sparks drift up behind the window |
| Count animation | in the terminal, items appear one after another and amounts count up |

*Subtle* turns on the first three, *rich* turns on all six.

The companion HUD is switched on and off in the [companion menu](07-companions.md#companion-menu).

## Tooltips

Hover over any mod item for a short description, and hold **Shift** to see its controls.

## JEI

With [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) installed:

- all recipes of the mod can be looked up,
- the `+` button in the Crafting Terminal transfers a recipe from your network and inventory into the grid,
- items without a recipe (boss loot, evolutions) have an info page that explains where they come from.

## Creative tab and advancements

The mod has its own creative tab and its own advancement tab, which leads you through the mod from your first
terminal to Cheldrogan.

## Sounds and languages

All helpers, companions, storm creatures and bosses have their own synthesized sounds in the mod's crystal style, with
subtitles. The mod is available in **English** and **German**.
