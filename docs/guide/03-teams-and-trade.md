# Teams and trade

## Team Terminal

Link a Team Terminal to your network with a bound Storage Key (right-click). It manages who may use the network.

- **Team name and color.** The color is shown on all helpers of the network, and the name appears in front of their
  name tags.
- **Access mode:** *private*, *team* or *public*.
- **Members with roles** (see below). Players who are not members get the role chosen for "public".
- **Channels:** team, trade, guard, storage and mail. Events of the network (a sale, an alarm of the Watch Moth, a full
  storage, an arriving parcel …) are posted to the chat of everyone who subscribed to the channel.

### Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| Guest | view the contents |
| Member | + take and store items, craft, use the wireless terminal |
| Builder | + link chests and helpers, use helpers |
| Manager | + upgrades, team settings, Trade Terminal, manage members up to Builder |
| Owner | everything, appoints managers, sets the access mode |

## Trade Terminal

A small shop for your network with **six offers** of the form *goods for price*.

- Managers set an offer by dropping items on its goods and price fields.
- **Anyone** can buy. The goods come straight from your storage and the payment goes into it.
- It keeps working **while you are offline**, as long as the base is loaded.
- Shift + click on *Buy* buys up to ten times in a row.
- Every card shows the remaining stock and how many of the price item the buyer carries.
